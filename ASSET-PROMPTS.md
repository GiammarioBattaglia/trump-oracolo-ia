# Illustrazioni di Trump & l’Oracolo IA

Create con lo strumento Imagegen integrato, usando la vignetta fornita dall’utente come riferimento. I file sono in assets/; ridimensionamento e conversione WebP/PNG servono alla pubblicazione web. La grafica è satirica.

## temple

Use case: illustration-story. Asset: landscape 16:9 background for a satirical browser game. Use the reference solely for character identity and painted editorial-caricature style. Create the same magnificent dark cathedral of artificial intelligence: gold and midnight navy, arches, candles, softly luminous data/server altar. A serene silver humanoid AI deity with elegant androgynous face sits on a server throne on the RIGHT third, with glowing circular gold-and-cyan halo, looking slightly down to the LEFT, one palm open. The LEFT half is EMPTY polished stone floor and soft dark architectural space to later overlay a kneeling game character; keep it visibly unoccupied. No Trump or any human in this background. Wide cinematic framing showing all throne and stage floor, rich gold/cyan light, refined illustrated detail. No text, no speech balloons, no UI, no logos, no watermarks. The central foreground floor is at the bottom 20% so a full body actor can be composited there.

## characters

Use case: illustration-story. Asset: ONE production game sprite atlas on genuinely TRANSPARENT background. Use the reference for the unmistakable satirical Trump caricature: swept blond hair, exaggerated expressive face, navy suit, very long red tie, same elegant painted comic illustration style. Make exactly 8 isolated FULL BODY character sprites arranged on a perfectly even 4-column by 2-row grid on a landscape 2:1 canvas, every cell square with at least 10% transparent padding, no overlaps, no labels, no backgrounds, no speech or lettering. Every sprite is fully visible, feet at same baseline, faces turned slightly RIGHT toward the deity, consistent scale. Row 1 column 1: Trump on both knees hands together praying, hopeful upward gaze, full black shoes and suit. Row 1 column 2: Trump transformed into a plump GREEN TOAD with unmistakable blond Trump hair, small long red necktie and hilarious offended eyebrows, whole toad. Row 1 column 3: Trump sitting defeated, slumped shoulders, holding a little white surrender flag, suit intact, harmless slapstick, no injuries or weapons. Row 1 column 4: Trump standing forlorn showing BOTH EMPTY POCKETS pulled inside out, long red tie, no money. Row 2 column 1: Trump on a ridiculously TINY cardboard throne holding a toy sceptre and crooked PAPER CROWN. Row 2 column 2: Trump in his suit wearing a large colourful DUNCE/JESTER cap, looking puzzled at an upside-down small book with BLANK pages. Row 2 column 3: Trump takes a theatrical bow to THREE small unimpressed ducks at his feet, all contained inside this one cell. Row 2 column 4: Trump transformed into a small round inflated BALLOON version of himself: recognisable blond head, little navy suit red tie, thin dangling limbs, comedic surprised face, no human holding it. Avoid realistic photomontage; explicitly a satirical game illustration. Preserve alpha transparency across the entire sheet.
